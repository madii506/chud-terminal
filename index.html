08 — FAQ

Questions chuds ask.

What is CHUD?

An onchain terminal for memecoin traders who never log off. You buy Solana memecoins and hedge them with perpetual futures on Hyperliquid, all from one page.

Does CHUD hold my money?

No. Your SOL and coins stay in your Solana wallet and every swap is signed there. Your margin stays in your own Hyperliquid account. There is no CHUD account and no deposit to us.

What is the session key?

Hyperliquid lets you approve an "agent" key that can place and cancel orders for your account but can never withdraw. CHUD creates one in your browser, your wallet approves it once, and it stays in this browser only. Forget it any time from the wallet menu.

What does hedging actually do?

A short gains when its price falls. If your coin and the perp fall together, the short pays back part of what the bag lost. If your coin pumps while the perp is flat, you keep most of the pump. The hedge costs you some upside and pays or earns funding.

My coin has no perp. Can I still hedge?

Yes, with SOL or another meme perp as a market hedge. It covers the "whole market dumps" risk, not the "this coin rugs" risk. The terminal says which one you're getting.

Which wallets work?

For memes: any Solana wallet that supports Wallet Standard, like Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. For hedges: any EVM wallet, like Phantom, MetaMask or Rabby, with USDC on Hyperliquid.

How do I fund Hyperliquid?

Deposit USDC on Hyperliquid's own app. If your USDC sits in your Hyperliquid spot balance, the terminal can move it to perps with one signature.

What does it cost?

Jupiter's swap fee and Hyperliquid's trading fee, the same as on their own sites. CHUD adds nothing.

What can go wrong?

Memecoins can go to zero. Leverage can be liquidated if the perp pumps far enough. Funding can turn against a short. A market hedge can fall less than your coin. Size it like you mean it, not like a chud.
